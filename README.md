# bloom

Welcome to Bloom 🌸

Do you feel a knot in your stomach when you have to speak to a stranger? Does the idea of a simple conversation feel like climbing a mountain? You aren't alone, and you don't have to face it all at once.

Bloom is a supportive companion designed to help you navigate social anxiety using the power of 'Graded Exposure.' Instead of forcing you into scary situations, Bloom breaks social interaction down into tiny, manageable 'micro-wins.'


## Getting Started

How it Works: 🌿 Grow Through Levels: Start as a Seedling with low-tension tasks (like a simple smile) and gradually evolve into a Sprout, Leaf, Stem, and finally, Bloom, as you tackle more challenging social goals.

🔥 Build Consistency: Use the Daily Streak system to turn bravery into a habit. Confidence is a muscle—the more you exercise it, the stronger it gets.

📈 Track Your Progress: Use the Confidence Meter to see your growth in real-time and the Reflection Journal to look back at your victories and realize how much you've improved.

🔒 Your Safe Space: Your journey is yours alone. All your data, reflections, and progress are stored locally on your device for total privacy.

It's time to stop hiding and start growing. Let's Bloom together.
